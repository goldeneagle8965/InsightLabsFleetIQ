The pitch in 30 seconds

  • Problem. Warehouse AMRs that need a central brain stall the whole floor when one robot, one Wi-Fi hop or one scheduler fails.
  • What we built. Each robot keeps its own state, broadcasts bids and intents, and picks its next move from what it hears back.
  • How it holds up. Packet loss, delay, blocked aisles and a single robot losing charge are all injected at runtime — the metrics come from the run.
  • Evidence. Every number on this page is read out of the live simulation or out of results/*.json; press Re-measure to rebuild it here.
